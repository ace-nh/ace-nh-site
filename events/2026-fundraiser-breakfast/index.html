Help NH students build something real

ACE mentors guide high school teams through a year-long design project covering architecture, construction, and engineering. No teaching experience needed.

How the Season Works

Four blocks, one project

Students work on their team project all year long. We teach four topic blocks, and each one pairs a teaching session with hands-on activity and project sessions.

Mid-Oct → Dec

Civil

1 teaching + 2 activity/project sessions

Mid-Oct → Dec

Architecture / Structure

1 teaching + 2 activity/project sessions

After break

Construction Management

1 teaching + 1–2 activity/project sessions

After break

MEP Systems

1 teaching + 1–2 activity/project sessions

Then students pick topics to dive deeper on, and we help them prep their final presentations.

  • Who can help: if you know a little more about construction than a high schooler, you're qualified. Help with basic software, answer questions, and run preplanned activities like connecting pipes or wiring circuits, plus team-building exercises.
  • When: sessions start around 2:30–3:00 PM.
  • Where: Tuesdays at Winnacunnet; Thursdays in Portsmouth and Manchester.
